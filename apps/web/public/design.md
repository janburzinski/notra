# Notra

## Overview

Notra is a GEO tool. It asks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI engines the questions a company's buyers ask, then shows whether the company appears, who appears instead, and how to fix it. This file covers the marketing site at usenotra.com. The dashboard has its own file at [app.usenotra.com/design.md](https://app.usenotra.com/design.md).

The site is light-first. A page opens on a rounded lavender wash with a slow violet dither, set in large Satoshi headlines, and then moves to white sections that show real product UI. Calls to action are pill-shaped violet gradient buttons. Inter carries all body copy, navigation, and UI.

## Color

### Violet

The action color is `#8B5CF6` (`primary`, `oklch(0.6056 0.2189 292.7172)`). It is used for links, focus rings, text selection (30% alpha), the primary CTA gradient, short emphasis words in headings (`<span class="text-primary">`), and the active thumb of segmented controls.

Deeper violets have fixed roles:

- `primary-hover` (`oklch(0.5 0.22 292.72)`) fills the announcement banner above the landing page.
- `#6D28D9` fills the featured pricing card and the badges on the other cards.
- `#5B21B6` colors footer column headings and footer link hover. In dark mode these use `#A78BFA`.

### Ink

Marketing text uses ink `#1E1E1E` at fixed alphas rather than `foreground` and `muted-foreground`:

| Alpha | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100% | `#1E1E1E` | Headlines, nav text, labels |
| 75% | `#1E1E1EBF` | Subheads, body paragraphs, footer links |
| 65% | `#1E1E1EA6` | Nav links on the scrolled island, footer legal |
| 60% | `#1E1E1E99` | Tertiary copy |
| 50% | `#1E1E1E80` | Icons, placeholders, social icons |
| 8% | `#1E1E1E14` | Hairline rings |

In dark mode ink becomes white at similar alphas (`text-white`, `text-white/70`, `text-white/60`, `text-white/50`).

### Lavender

The Notra mark's lavender `#C8B2EE` is also the site's atmosphere color:

- `#C8B2EE40` (25%) fills the hero, page heroes, the CTA banner, and the nav mega cards.
- `#C8B2EE26` (15%) is the hover fill for nav items and the mobile menu button.
- `#C8B2EE1F` (12%) tints callout cards, for example on `/about`.

In dark mode the washes become solid deep violets: `#2A2140` for heroes, `#231D3A` for the CTA banner, and `#141019` for the footer.

### Logo colors

`lavender` `#C8B2EE` is the mark's fill and `ink` `#1E1E1E` its stroke. On dark surfaces the mark sits on a `cream` `#F6F3F1` tile.

### Theme tokens

The semantic tokens (`background`, `card`, `muted`, `border` and the rest) follow the shadcn roles listed above. Product mockups and tables embedded in marketing pages use them, together with the dualtone `shell` and `table-*` tokens shared with the dashboard. Dark mode keeps the same roles on `hsl(233 7% 8%)`. Its `input` is `hsl(240 4% 20%)`, and its chart colors switch to a violet ramp.

## Layout

- Spacing follows Tailwind's 4px scale.
- Landing sections sit in `max-w-360` (1440px) with `px-6`, `sm:px-12` and `lg:px-20`. Top padding grows from 64–80px on mobile to 140–160px on `lg`.
- The hero, page heroes, and footer are inset 24px from the viewport (`px-6 pt-6`), so the wash reads as a rounded panel rather than a full-bleed band.
- Wide blocks such as the CTA banner and footer content cap at `max-w-[87rem]` (1392px). Legal prose caps at 720px.
- Pages are product-led. Feature cards and the answer section show real dashboard modules (`MockFrame` wraps `InstrumentSection` from `@notra/ui`) in place of abstract illustrations.
- Below-the-fold landing sections use `content-visibility: auto` with an intrinsic size.

## Elevation and atmosphere

- The violet dither is the site's one atmospheric effect. It is the `Dithering` shader from `@paper-design/shaders-react` in its wave shape (`type="4x4"`, `size` 2.9) at 18–50% violet alpha, rendered by `DeferredDithering`. It appears on the landing hero, page and integration heroes, the CTA banner, the footer, the featured pricing card, testimonials, blog cards, numbered step cards, and callouts. Every instance goes through `DeferredDithering`. It mounts lazily and fades in once painted. With `prefers-reduced-motion` it is replaced by a static 4px dot grid.
- Raised surfaces combine a 1px ring (`#ECECEC` or `#1E1E1E14`) with a soft drop shadow such as `0 1px 2px #28282814`. Floating chrome uses a wider shadow, `0 2px 20px #1E1E1E14`.
- Dark mode drops most marketing shadows (`dark:shadow-none`) and uses `ring-white/10` for edges. The theme also sets every `--shadow-*` token to zero in dark mode.
- Gradients are limited to the CTA button fills, the white-to-gray nav panels and toggle tracks, the lavender hover on nav mega cards, the feature card tint, and the footer wash. Do not add gradient blobs or orbs.

## Shapes

- Radius tokens derive from `--radius`: 10px in light mode and 8px in dark mode, so `sm`, `md`, `lg` and `xl` are 2px tighter in dark mode.
- Pills (`rounded-full`) are the most common shape: CTAs, toggles, badges, the hero URL field, and nav link hovers.
- Large panels are rounded: `rounded-3xl` (24px) for heroes, pricing cards, and the footer top; 25px for the CTA banner; 22px for nav dropdown panels; `rounded-2xl` (16px) for cards, product frames, and mega cards; 13px for feature cards.
- Kit buttons use a 12px radius with `corner-shape: squircle`. `[data-slot="button"]` gets `corner-squircle` globally.

## Components

**CTA button.** `CtaButton` from `@notra/ui` is the marketing call to action. It is a `rounded-full` pill in three variants:

- `primary`: a violet oklab gradient with an 8px violet halo.
- `flat`: the same gradient without the halo.
- `light`: a white-to-gray gradient with an `#ECECEC` ring.

Hover deepens the bottom gradient stop over 200ms. Sizes are `sm` 40px, `default` 44px and `lg` 48px. The focus ring is 3px at `ring/50`. Hero CTAs override the type to Satoshi 18px with a 41px radius. CTAs come in pairs: `primary` first, then `light` ("Start for free" and "Book a Call").

**Kit button.** `Button` from `@notra/ui` is for free tools (QR code, UTM builder, thread builder), product mockups, and small utility controls such as copy, download, and the theme toggle. Its default height is 32px. It uses a Depth gradient, a 12px squircle, and presses to `scale(0.96)`.

**Navigation.** The bar floats over the hero, transparent and wide (1295px), 40px from the top, or 80px on the landing page, where the announcement banner sits above it. After 64px of scroll it morphs into a white island: 1024px max width, 64px tall, 16px radius, 16px from the top, with the floating shadow. Dropdowns open 22px-radius panels that resize between groups. Product groups show lavender mega cards. Sign up is a white 32px squircle pill with a keyboard hint (`Kbd`, 20px, `rounded-md`).

**Hero.** A `rounded-3xl` lavender wash with the dither. It contains a centered Satoshi `h1` that cycles AI engine names (morphing text plus engine icons), an ink-75 subhead, either a pill URL field with an inline CTA or a CTA pair, and a live demo below. `MarketingHeroWash` is the same shell for inner pages, with optional `media` and `backdrop`.

**Feature card.** A 13px-radius card with a faint lavender gradient and an `#ECECEC` ring. It holds an Inter title, a `#6A6B70` description, and a product mockup faded out at the bottom with a mask.

**Pricing.** A pill billing toggle with a gradient-primary thumb that springs (`SPRING.snappy`) between options. Plan cards are `rounded-3xl` on `#F7F7F7`. The featured plan is `#6D28D9` with a lavender dither and a `light` CTA. Other plans use the `primary` CTA. **Prose.** Legal pages use `prose prose-neutral` at 720px. Headings are Satoshi 500 with `-0.02em` tracking. Paragraphs are ink 75% at a 28px line height. Links are `primary` with no underline until hover.

**Icons.** Hugeicons (`@hugeicons/react`), `currentColor`, usually 16–24px. Engine and brand marks keep their own artwork.

## Motion

Use the shared scale from `@notra/ui`: `instant` 100ms, `fast` 150ms, `normal` 200ms, `slow` 300ms, `slower` 500ms. The default curve is the strong ease-out `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. Springs are `indicator`, `indicatorFlat`, `snappy` and `gentle`. View transitions run 420ms on `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`.

The navbar morph uses `tween("slow", "emphasized")`. The hero cycles its engine word every 2.6s. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`: the cycle stops, the dither becomes static, and transitions drop to the end state.

## Voice

Copy is concrete and about AI visibility: "Be the answer when buyers ask ChatGPT.", "Find out what AI agents say about you and change it.", "Add a few prompts and run a scan to read the answers."

- Name the engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok) and the outcome (appear, get cited, show up) rather than abstract benefits.
- CTA labels are short: "Start for free", "Book a Call", "Check your AI visibility".
- Avoid generic AI marketing words such as "revolutionize", "unlock", "supercharge", "seamless" and "effortless".

## Do's and don'ts

- Use `CtaButton` for marketing actions such as signup, contact, and install. Use the kit `Button` for tool UI and utility controls.
- Use Satoshi (`font-display`) for display type and Inter for everything else. Keep Instrument Serif off page titles.
- Use ink alphas for marketing text and the semantic tokens for embedded product UI.
- Get atmosphere only from the lavender wash and the violet dither (`DeferredDithering`). Do not add other background effects.
- Show real product modules as proof.
- Keep violet for actions, links, selection, and short emphasis. Do not set long text in violet.
- Do not add new accent hues or gradient blobs.
- Do not center long-form prose.
- Do not remove visible focus states. CTAs and toggles use a 3px `ring/50`.
