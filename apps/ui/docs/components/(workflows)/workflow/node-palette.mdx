<RegistryInstall name="workflow-node-palette" />

`WorkflowNodePalette` groups `blocks` by their `group` and filters them as you type. Clicking a block calls `onAdd`. Dragging one onto a `WorkflowCanvas` with `onDropBlock` drops it where you let go. `WORKFLOW_BLOCKS` ships triggers, AI models, logic, deterministic tasks, integrations and email steps for GEO work.
