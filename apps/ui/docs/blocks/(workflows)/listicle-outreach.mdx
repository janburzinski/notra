Best-of listicles are some of the pages answer engines quote most, so getting onto them moves AI visibility. This block lists the ones that matter, with the engines citing each page, the competitors already on it and where your outreach stands. Click a row for the details sheet, or Pitch to write the owner an email with an AI draft you can rewrite, preview and send. Sent pitches move into the sequence.

Built from the [Outreach components](/components/outreach/prospect-table) and the [Workflow logos](/components/workflow/logo).
